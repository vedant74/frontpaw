FAQ

Common questions.

Will it sound like a robot? +

No. Frontpaw uses production-grade voice models that callers consistently can't tell apart from a human. We disclose AI status when asked, and escalate to your staff for any urgent or sensitive call.

How long does setup take? +

One day for clinics on ezyVet or Vetspire. About a week for Cornerstone or AVImark (we use a CSV-export workflow until partner API access lands). You forward your existing number to us, we don't replace it.

What if it books wrong? +

For the first 90 days, every booking is reviewed by your staff before it's written into the PMS. Once we've proven accuracy, you can flip on autonomous mode. You're always in control.

What does it cost? +

Design partner clinics get a free 4-week pilot. After that, $500/mo flat for the wedge product, with optional recovery contingency for higher tiers. We'll walk through specifics on the demo.

Will it replace my front desk? +

No. Frontpaw handles overflow, recall, and after-hours — the calls your team is missing today. Your staff stays focused on the pets and people in front of them.
